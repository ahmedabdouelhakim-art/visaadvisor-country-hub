## Source precedence

1. Controlling official authority.
2. Official operational source.
3. Multilateral or statistical authority.
4. Peer-reviewed or audited independent evidence.
5. Reputable reporting for context.
6. Community and personal material for discovery or explicitly labelled experience only.

Mirrors are distribution copies, not independent corroboration. Personal documents
are excluded from the evidence store.
