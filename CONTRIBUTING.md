# Corrections and contributions

Country Hub welcomes evidence-led corrections and carefully scoped additions. It
is not a forum for individual immigration, medical, legal, tax, or financial advice.

## A useful correction includes

1. The affected record ID, place ID, or field.
2. The current value and the proposed replacement.
3. A direct authoritative source URL and precise evidence scope.
4. The source publisher, jurisdiction, and date checked.
5. The effective date when it differs from the check date.
6. An explanation of whether the source supports, qualifies, or contradicts the field.

## Evidence rules

- Prefer the controlling official authority for high-stakes rules.
- An operational source may qualify implementation but must not silently replace the controlling authority.
- Do not generalize from a single traveller's experience.
- Do not submit passport scans, identity numbers, booking references, addresses, phone numbers, or other personal data.
- Keep maximum stay, authorisation validity, and period actually granted separate.
- Preserve conflicts and historical releases; do not rewrite published history silently.
- Blank means not supplied or not supported. It does not mean none.

## Review and release

Accepted material corrections create a new record revision, change event, and
semantic release when they affect a published artifact. High-stakes changes
require evidence review and separate publishing approval.

Open a GitHub issue without personal data, or email [help@visaadvisor.ai](mailto:help@visaadvisor.ai)
with the subject `Country Hub sourced correction`.
