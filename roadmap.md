- Verify capital, languages, currencies, IANA timezones, national emergency contacts, and official tourism structure.

## v0.3.0 — entry-policy normalization

- Create a complete Egyptian ordinary-passport traveller profile.
- Split each legacy `stay_or_validity` value into stay and authorisation validity when official wording supports it.
- Split core requirements into atomic requirement rows.

- Record arrival mode, purpose, entries allowed, and explicit exceptions.

- Extend structured country basics, cities, cost, safety, connectivity, and healthcare across all 12 sovereign states.
- Add field-level bilingual review and parity checks.
- Add freshness/conflict dashboard and change-detection queue.
