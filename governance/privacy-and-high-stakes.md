# Privacy and high-stakes safeguards

## Public-data boundary

Never include the following in the public repository, evidence register, API, screenshots, examples, or release archives:

- passport or identity-card images;
- passport, DNI, CUIL/CUIT, tax, or personal-document numbers;
- full dates of birth;
- signatures, biometrics, or machine-readable zones;
- private addresses, phone numbers, authentication material, or account recovery data;
- unpublished private correspondence unless explicitly consented and safely redacted.

Personal documents shared during operations are not research evidence and must not be copied into Country Hub. This rule applies even when a document supports a real identity or nationality.

## High-stakes domains

Entry, immigration, tax, health, safety, legal, and financial fields require:

- current official primary evidence;
- explicit jurisdiction and traveller conditions;
- visible verification and review dates;
- conditional wording, never a guarantee of boarding, admission, treatment, residency, or tax outcome;
- a direct official verification/action link;
- safe publication blocking when evidence is stale, expired, missing, or disputed.

Airlines, transit airports, and border authorities may apply separate checks. Stored information never replaces a current official check or qualified professional advice.

## Personal experience

First-person stories may enrich editorial pages when they are genuine, consented, dated, and clearly labelled. They cannot prove universal safety, friendliness, entry eligibility, cost, or service quality.

## AI boundary

AI may help structure, translate, compare, or flag potential changes. It may not invent missing data, claim a source was checked when it was not, upgrade evidence status, fabricate experience, or give final approval to high-stakes facts.
